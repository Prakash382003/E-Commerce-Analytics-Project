# Marketplace Analysis — End-to-End E-Commerce Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end **Marketplace Analysis** project focused on understanding customer behavior, sales performance, products, sellers, payments, reviews, returns, and delivery operations.

The project uses **SQL, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI** to transform raw marketplace data into meaningful business insights and an interactive dashboard.

---

## Project Objectives

* Analyze customer demographics and customer growth.
* Understand sales and order performance.
* Analyze product categories, brands, and pricing.
* Evaluate seller performance and seller ratings.
* Understand payment method usage.
* Analyze customer review ratings.
* Identify return patterns and return status.
* Analyze delivery performance and shipping status.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Generate actionable business insights from the analysis.

---

## Dataset

The marketplace dataset contains:

* **20,000 Customers**
* **50,000 Orders**
* **2,000 Products**
* **300 Sellers**
* **28,000 Reviews**
* **7,000 Returns**
* Related payment and shipping data.

The dataset was analyzed across multiple business areas to provide an end-to-end view of marketplace operations.

## Business Insights

### 1. Strong Overall Order Delivery Performance

Out of **50,000 orders**, **41,011 orders were delivered**, representing approximately **82% of total orders**.

This indicates that the majority of orders successfully reached customers, while the remaining orders were distributed across shipped and cancelled statuses.

### 2. Sales Volume Shows a Large Marketplace Transaction Base

The marketplace generated approximately **₹1.18 billion in total sales** across **50,000 orders**.

The calculated **Average Order Value (AOV) is approximately ₹23.65K per order**, providing a useful metric for evaluating order-level revenue performance.

### 3. Customer Base Is Predominantly Male

The dataset contains:

* **11,548 Male customers**
* **8,452 Female customers**

This means male customers represent approximately **57.7%** of the customer base.

This distribution can help businesses understand their current customer composition when planning segmentation and marketing analysis.

### 4. Customer Acquisition Was Highest in 2025

Customer joining data shows:

* 2022 — 4,846
* 2023 — 4,864
* 2024 — 4,854
* **2025 — 4,911**
* 2026 — 525

Among the completed years, **2025 recorded the highest number of new customer joins**.

### 5. Customer Reviews Are Concentrated Around High Ratings

The review dataset contains **28,000 reviews**.

* 5-star — 12,581
* 4-star — 9,767
* 3-star — 3,348
* 2-star — 1,440
* 1-star — 864

Approximately **80% of reviews are 4-star or 5-star**, indicating that high ratings form the majority of customer feedback.

### 6. Most Returns Are Approved or Processed

Out of **7,000 returns**:

* Approved — 3,878
* Processed — 2,428
* Rejected — 694

Approximately **90% of return records are either approved or processed**, while around **10% are rejected**.

This provides useful visibility into the marketplace's return-handling process.

### 7. Seller Ratings Are Generally High

The overall average seller rating is approximately **4.10 out of 5**.

This provides an overall view of seller quality and customer perception across the marketplace.
